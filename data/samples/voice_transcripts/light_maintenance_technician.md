# Voice note - chasing spindle vibration

"Before you open the guard, stop the program and put the safety lock on. No
shortcuts.

First check the chuck jaws for chips. Clean them and tighten to the torque on
the panel, not more. Then look at the tool holder. If the insert is chipped,
replace it; a chipped insert sounds like a bearing problem but it is not.

Run the spindle at 500 rpm with no load. Listen. If it is smooth at no load but
vibrates under load, it is usually the chuck or the tool. If it vibrates at no
load, it is the bearing or the belt.

If it still vibrates, log SP-402 and call maintenance. Do not keep running it."
