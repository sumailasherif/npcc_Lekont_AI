# Voice note - clearing an overlock jam

"So, when the overlock jams, the first thing is the red button. Stop the machine
and wait. Don't rush to put your hand in while it is still moving. That is how
people get hurt.

Then lift the presser foot. There is a small guard over the needle, switch that
off. Use the curved tweezers, the ones in the drawer, and cut the thread. Never
pull the thread through the needle, it bends the needle and then you have a
bigger problem.

Look at the tension discs. If there is lint, brush it out. The brush is hanging
on the side. After that, re-thread following the yellow marks. Yellow, then the
looper, then the needle.

Close the guard and sew on a scrap piece. If the seam is not clean, check the
tension again before you run production.

Enn kitchoz: pa fer vite. Si ou pa sir, apel mwa."
