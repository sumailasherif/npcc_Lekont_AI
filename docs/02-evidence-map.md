# Evidence map

Source: NPCC research brief, "What Mauritian companies need from AI" (12 October
2026), based on nine HRDC reports, 2020-2026. Codes S1-S9 follow that brief.
Figures describe survey or consultation samples, not national averages.

## Cross-sector needs to features

| Need (reported) | Evidence | Product feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skills are the brake on technology adoption | S1, S2, S6, S7, S8 | Capture and multilingual instructions reduce the skills required to use existing equipment |
| Weak "middle layer" of supervisors, technicians, maintenance staff | S6, S7, S8 | Supervisor-authored SOPs and gap-driven coaching |
| Maintenance technicians hard to recruit and retain | S6, S7, S8 | Capture expert diagnostic know-how before it leaves |
| Quality, safety and compliance pressure | S6, S7, S8 | Approved, versioned instructions with citations and an audit trail |
| Multilingual workforce and expatriate operators | S4, S6, S7 | EN / FR / Kreol output, Kreol reviewed by a supervisor |
| SMEs need phased, modular, practical support | S2, S6 | Sector packs, Excel/phone-first, no new hardware |
| Knowing what to automate / what to train | S1, S2, S5 | Gap report shows what operators actually cannot do |
| Productivity is the pitch | S1, S2 | Time-to-competency and coaching hours, not "AI" |

## Sector snapshots

| Sector | Pain reported | How the product responds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Textile and apparel (S6) | Shrinking, ageing workforce; foreign labour; downtime; quality inconsistency; traceability and ESG | Multilingual instructions for operators; capture retiring experts; approved traceability steps |
| Food manufacturing (S7) | Input costs; food safety and export compliance; shortage of technical and supervisory staff; dual-speed adoption | HACCP/traceability SOPs with approval and versioning; gap-driven coaching |
| Light engineering (S8) | Shortage of problem-solvers; fault diagnosis, calibration, documentation; costly equipment; succession risk | Capture diagnostic know-how; fault-code-linked instructions; role skills map |

## What the evidence does not say

- It is not an AI needs survey. The mapping from need to AI feature is the
  team's interpretation.
- The only direct AI survey (S1) is from 2021, covers ICT and finance, and
  pre-dates generative AI.
- The 2026 reports are qualitative consultations, not statistical surveys.
- There is little evidence on tourism, agriculture, retail, public services or
  the ocean economy. Do not claim those markets without interviews.
