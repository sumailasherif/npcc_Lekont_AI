# Evidence map

Source: NPCC/HRDC research brief, *What Mauritian companies need from AI*
(12 October 2026), based on S1–S9.

## Findings → need → feature

| Source | Finding | Need | Lekont feature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 (2021, n=94) | 62% said new technology was "not relevant"; 11% unclear when to use automation; 4% unsure what it does | Explain the value and the starting point | Readiness diagnostic with effort/impact and next steps |
| S1 (n=40) | Lack of skills among current staff 55%; no local training 53%; lack of funds 40% | Tool must work for existing staff, low cost | Plain UI, no SQL, CSV export, free-tier stack |
| S1 (n=105) | Productivity/efficiency 53%, quality 20%, cost 8% | Sell time saved and errors avoided | "Fields extracted without correction", review queue, hours saved |
| S1 (n=38 AI users) | Automate processes 79%, insights 58%, prediction 52% | Automation + insight | Extraction + ask-your-spreadsheet |
| S1 (n=37) | Benefits: save time/money 79%, productivity 76%, faster decisions 73% | Speed to first value | Extract in seconds; answer in one question |
| S2 (2022) | 38% no automated processes, 48% up to half; only 40% have an automation strategy; 11% unclear when to automate | Diagnostic and phasing | Readiness scores and a recommended first process |
| S2 | Drivers: productivity >70%, less downtime >60%, consistency >50%; barriers: skills 29%, funding ~25%, structure ~25% | Low-cost, modular, skills-light | Free stack; six processes; no new role required |
| S4 (2026) | BPO wants less routine execution; demand for BI, RPA, ERP, Power BI, document handling | Back-office automation with analytics | Invoice/payslip extraction + BI answers |
| S5 (2026) | Priority areas: document digitisation, OCR, electronic document management, ERP integration, AI/ML | Document intake and export | Field extraction, review, CSV export, ERP-ready shape |
| S3 (2020) | 1,326 enterprises, 10 sectors; routine work shifting | Broad SME relevance | Sector-agnostic design; distributor demo |

## Evidence strength

- **#5: strong** — four reports point at document handling and back-office work.
- **#7: medium** — three reports, and the need is inferred from "unclear when to
  automate" plus the skills barrier. The demo compensates by making the
  recommendation explainable rather than predictive.

## What the evidence does *not* say

- It does not ask SMEs "would you pay for this?" → validate in interviews.
- It does not cover retail, tourism or agriculture → keep the product
  sector-agnostic and test with the first ten interviews.
- The 2021 AI numbers predate generative AI → do not present them as current
  adoption.
