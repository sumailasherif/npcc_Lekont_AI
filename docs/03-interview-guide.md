# Stage 1 interview guide

Target: 8–10 interviews with SME owners, accountants and BPO finance leads in
Mauritius. 25–30 minutes. Ask about the last time, not opinions.

## Warm-up

1. What does your business do, and how many people handle paperwork?
2. Which documents arrive every week — invoices, payslips, contracts, others?
   In which languages?

## The last document

3. Walk me through the last invoice you received, from arrival to filing. Who
   touched it, and where did it end up?
4. How long did that take? What did you have to retype?
5. When was the last time a document was lost, misfiled or paid twice? What
   happened?

## Numbers and decisions

6. If you wanted to know your spend with your top supplier last quarter, how
   would you get that number today? How long would it take?
7. What do you do with the answer — decide, report, send it to the bank or the
   accountant?
8. Which number do you not trust today, and why?

## Automation

9. Have you tried to automate any of this before? What stopped it?
10. If you could automate one process next month, which one — and what would
    make you say it worked?
11. What would you not be willing to share with a tool, and why?

## Close

12. Who else should I speak to?
13. May I show you a prototype and watch you use it for ten minutes?

## What to listen for

- The number of systems a single invoice touches.
- The name of the person who actually retypes data.
- Whether the pain is time, errors, cash, or audit fear.
- The price anchor: what they already pay an accountant or a software licence.
- Any mention of French/English mixing, or of WhatsApp as a workflow.
